Who am I

A student of mechatronics engineering at ENISO in Sousse, Tunisia, I am in my final year. By nature I am a curious and critical thinker. My approach is to get to the bottom of a problem before doing anything about it, and I work in an iterative fashion: put something to the test, see where it falls short and make the necessary improvements. How I think when I work on a problem.

  • I get to the root of a problem

    I look for the cause before I look for a fix. A solution that works without my understanding why tends to fail again later, so I keep questioning the result until I can explain it.

  • I work in iterations

    I put a first version to the test, look at where it falls short, improve it and test again. I trust small, repeated improvements more than one large attempt.

  • I learn by building, and say what is tested

    ROS2, SLAM and Nav2 are not part of my school curriculum, so I learned them by building a robot with them. My robots run in simulation so far, and I have yet to try them on hardware.

Aug 2026, Nexor Robotics internship, simulation

LLM coordination of an AGV fleet

An operator types a mission in plain language. An LLM splits it into tasks and assigns them to a fleet of 2 to 6 AGVs, based on each robot's position and battery level.

Every plan goes through a verifier first (strict JSON schema, zone conflicts, low battery, unreachable zones), so the model never drives the robots directly. When an AGV runs low or fails, its tasks move to the others.

I also designed the AGV from scratch: CAD chassis, URDF/Xacro, 2D LiDAR, camera and odometry, navigated with Nav2.

2025, Webots simulation

Vision-guided pick and place

Parts arrive on a conveyor in random orientations. A YOLOv8 oriented-bounding-box model I trained gives each part's angle, the UR10e lines up its wrist, and a Robotiq 3-finger gripper picks it off the belt and drops it in a bin.

Inverse kinematics solved analytically, YOLO running in a separate Python server over TCP, and a state machine with retries, reconnection and watchdog timeouts so the cell recovers on its own instead of freezing.

  • YOLOv8 OBB
  • Python
  • Webots

  • Inverse kinematics
